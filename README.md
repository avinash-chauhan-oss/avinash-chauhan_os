## About Me

I am a BS-MS student specializing in **Integrated Mathematics (i2)** at IISER Thiruvananthapuram. I bridge rigorous mathematical frameworks with practical engineering applications, with a focus on **quantitative finance**, **stochastic processes**, and **machine learning**.

My research and projects span:
- 📈 **Algorithmic Trading** — RL agents for optimal market making (SAC/PPO vs. Avellaneda-Stoikov)
- ⚛️ **Quantum ML** — Classical-quantum hybrid classifiers using PennyLane
- 🦠 **Computational Biology** — Metabolic modeling of gut microbiome communities (WSAI, IIT Madras)
- 🧮 **Mathematical Modeling** — ODE systems, stochastic processes, dynamical systems
